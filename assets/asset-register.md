# Asset Register - TechNova Solutions



**Project:** Mini Compliance Program

**Frameworks:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5

**Document Owner:** GRC Analyst

**Version:** 1.0

**Assessment Date:** 10 November 2026



## 1. Purpose



The purpose of this asset register is to identify and document the information assets, hardware, software, cloud services and business systems used by TechNova Solutions.



Maintaining an asset inventory helps the organization understand its information security risks and apply appropriate security controls.

## 3. Asset Classification



| Classification | Meaning |

|---|---|

| Public | Information approved for public access |

| Internal | Information intended for company employees |

| Confidential | Sensitive company information requiring restricted access |

| Restricted | Highly sensitive information requiring strict access controls |



## 4. Asset Management Responsibilities



- IT Manager: Maintains hardware, network and infrastructure inventory.

- Cloud Administrator: Manages cloud infrastructure and storage.

- HR Manager: Maintains employee information assets.

- Finance Manager: Protects financial records.

- Development Manager: Manages source code and applications.

- Data Protection Officer: Oversees customer data protection.



## 5. Security Requirements



1. Maintain an updated inventory of company assets.

2. Assign an owner to every important asset.

3. Classify information according to sensitivity.

4. Restrict access based on business requirements.

5. Review asset ownership and inventory periodically.

6. Securely dispose of assets and information when no longer required.



## 6. Compliance Mapping



- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A Control 5.9 - Inventory of information and other associated assets.

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A Control 5.12 - Classification of information.

- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 CM-8 - System Component Inventory.
