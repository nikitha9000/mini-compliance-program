## 1. Introduction



TechNova Solutions is a fictional technology company that uses cloud infrastructure, business applications and digital information systems to support its operations.



This Mini Compliance Program establishes a structured approach to identifying information security risks, defining security controls and improving compliance with recognized cybersecurity frameworks.



## 2. Purpose



The purpose of this program is to:



Protect company and customer information.



Identify and manage cybersecurity risks.



Establish security policies and procedures.



Define control ownership and accountability.



Evaluate security control maturity.



Maintain evidence of security activities.



Support continuous compliance improvement.

## 4. Applicable Frameworks



ISO/IEC 27001:2022



The primary framework for establishing and improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).



Relevant areas include:



Information security policies.



Asset management.



Access control.



Risk management.



Incident management.



Business continuity.



Logging and monitoring.



NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5



A supporting security control reference used to identify and map technical and organizational safeguards.



Relevant control families include:



Access Control (AC).



Identification and Authentication (IA).



Audit and Accountability (AU).



Configuration Management (CM).



Incident Response (IR).



System and Information Integrity (SI).

## 6. Scope Exclusions



The following areas are excluded from this initial simulated exercise:



Physical security inspections of actual office locations.



Independent penetration testing.



Third-party supplier audits.



Formal certification audits.



Verification of live AWS production infrastructure.



Legal and regulatory compliance assessments beyond the defined exercise scope.



These exclusions may be considered in future compliance assessments.

## 8. Success Criteria



The compliance program will be considered successfully established for this exercise when:



An asset register is documented.



At least 15 security controls are identified and mapped.



Control owners are assigned.



Control maturity is assessed.



A risk register is maintained.



Evidence requirements are documented.



A compliance summary is prepared.



Improvement actions are identified.
